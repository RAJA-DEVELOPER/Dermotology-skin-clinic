Pricing & Policy FAQ

Everything you need to know before booking your treatment.

Medical dermatology treatments (such as acne, eczema, and skin cancer screenings) may be covered by health insurance. Aesthetic and cosmetic procedures are generally not covered. We recommend checking with your insurer prior to booking. Our patient coordinators can provide detailed cost breakdowns and help you understand your coverage.

Yes! We offer flexible 0% interest payment plans through our financing partners for treatments over $500. Plans can be spread across 3, 6, or 12 months. We also accept all major credit cards, HSA/FSA funds, and bank transfers. Ask about our financing options when you book your consultation.

Your first consultation is completely free when you book a treatment package. Standalone consultations are priced at $75 for a 45-minute session. This fee is fully credited toward any treatment you proceed with. Follow-up consultations are complimentary for all active patients within their treatment programme.

We kindly ask for at least 24 hours' notice for cancellations or rescheduling. Cancellations with less than 24 hours' notice may incur a $50 late-cancellation fee. No-shows are charged 50% of the treatment cost. Exceptions are made for genuine medical emergencies. You can reschedule via our online portal, by phone, or WhatsApp at no charge with adequate notice.

Pre-treatment care varies by procedure. Generally, avoid sun exposure for 2 weeks prior, discontinue retinoids 5â€“7 days before, and arrive with clean, make-up-free skin. Post-treatment, follow the specific aftercare protocol provided by your dermatologist. This typically includes sun protection (SPF 50+), gentle moisturisation, and avoiding heat/exercise for 24â€“48 hours. Detailed written aftercare instructions are provided after every session.
