Frequently Asked Questions
Is parking available?
Yes! All three locations offer free parking. The Downtown clinic has a dedicated 40-space lot accessible from Medical Boulevard. Westside and North Shore have on-site parking as well.
Do you accept insurance?
We accept most major insurance plans including Aetna, Blue Cross Blue Shield, Cigna, UnitedHealthcare, and Medicare. Please call us or check with your insurer ahead of your visit to confirm coverage.
Do you accept walk-ins?
We accommodate walk-in patients for urgent skin concerns subject to availability. For cosmetic and elective treatments, we recommend booking in advance to ensure you get your preferred doctor and time slot.
Is the clinic wheelchair accessible?
All DermaCare locations are fully ADA-compliant. We have wide-doorway entrances, elevator access, accessible restrooms, and staff trained to assist patients with mobility needs.
What payment methods are accepted?
We accept cash, all major credit/debit cards, HSA/FSA cards, and offer 0% financing plans through CareCredit for treatments over $500. Ask our front desk team for details.

Before You Visit

Please bring a valid photo ID, your insurance card, a list of current medications and supplements, any previous dermatology records or biopsy results, and your completed new-patient intake form (available online). Arrive 10 minutes early to allow time for check-in.
All three DermaCare locations offer free on-site parking. The Downtown clinic is also accessible via subway (Lines A, C, E at 34th Street). Westside Center is served by the M23 bus. For turn-by-turn directions, use the "Get Directions" buttons in the Locations section above.
Your first visit typically lasts 45–60 minutes. After check-in, our nursing team will take your skin history and photographs for our VISIA® digital analysis system. Your dermatologist will then perform a thorough consultation, review your concerns, and discuss a personalised treatment plan. You'll leave with a written care plan and all your questions answered.
We kindly ask for 24 hours' notice if you need to cancel or reschedule your appointment. Cancellations made with less than 24 hours' notice, or no-shows, may incur a $50 cancellation fee. We understand emergencies happen — please call us as soon as possible and we will do our best to accommodate you.
