Established 2004 • 20+ Years of Excellence

Our Story of Excellence in Dermatology

Since 2004, DermaCare has stood at the forefront of evidence-based dermatological care. What began as a single-room practice led by Dr. Sarah Mitchell has grown into a multi-location center of clinical excellence — trusted by over 5,000 patients and home to 15 board-certified specialists. Every protocol we offer is rooted in peer-reviewed science, ensuring outcomes that are not just visible, but lasting.

Our patient-first philosophy means every consultation begins with listening. We believe beautiful, healthy skin is achievable for everyone, regardless of skin type, tone, or background. From the moment you step through our doors, you experience a clinic where clinical precision meets genuine compassion — and where your skin's long-term health is always the priority.
