How It Works

Your Journey to Clear Skin

Our structured five-step process ensures every patient receives a fully personalised, evidence-driven treatment experience from start to finish.

1
Consultation

Meet your dedicated dermatologist for an in-depth discussion of your skin history, concerns, and goals.

2
Skin Analysis

VISIA® digital complexion analysis reveals subsurface concerns invisible to the naked eye for precise diagnosis.

3
Custom Plan

Your specialist designs a bespoke treatment plan with a clear timeline, goals, and expected outcomes.

4
Treatment Sessions

Scheduled in-clinic treatments using FDA-approved technology, carried out in a sterile, comfortable environment.

5
Follow-up Care

Post-treatment review appointments and a maintenance skincare routine to protect and sustain your results.
